Privacy Policy

Learn how Budgeting App handles and protects your information.

Last Updated: August 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Budgeting App. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the measures taken to protect your information.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • Financial information that you choose to enter, such as income, expenses and budgets.
  • Profile information that you voluntarily provide.
  • Messages and information submitted through our Contact Us form.
  • Application usage information required to improve the user experience.

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to the Budgeting App may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Display your income, expenses and budget information.
  • Generate financial summaries and analytics.
  • Provide budgeting and financial insights.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve application functionality and performance.

Your Financial Data

Your financial information is used to provide budgeting, tracking and analytical features. You should only enter information that you are comfortable storing within the application.

Never enter banking passwords, card PINs, OTPs or other highly sensitive authentication credentials into the Budgeting App.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with your account from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to:

  • Provide application functionality.
  • Respond to legal or regulatory requirements.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the application.

Cookies and Local Storage

The application may use browser storage mechanisms, such as local storage, to maintain preferences and application data during development or local usage.

These mechanisms help provide a consistent user experience and may be replaced or supplemented by server-side storage when the backend is integrated.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and regulations, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to your information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, its features or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will include a revised "Last Updated" date.

Questions About Privacy?

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
